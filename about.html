The picture is decided before the shutter.

Marlowe Adeyemi-Voss photographs clothing as material: how a fabric takes a hard light, where it gives it back, what it hides. The work is planned on paper first, in lighting plots and call sheets, so the day itself can be quiet.

The studio is fictional, invented for a web design portfolio. The pictures are licensed stock photographs standing in for the work; credits are listed below.

  1. One light first.

    Build the picture with a single key. Add a second only when the first has been exhausted.

  2. Fabric leads.

    The garment sets the angle. Satin gets a long rake, knit gets a low side light, foil gets a flag.

  3. Numbered, always.

    Every frame keeps its number from the plot to the print, so any picture can be found again.

Concept notice

Marlowe Adeyemi-Voss, the clients, garments and locations on this site are fictional. There are no real testimonials, clients or results. Nothing submitted through the booking form is sent or stored.
