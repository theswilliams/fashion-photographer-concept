## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is a shoot-day call sheet. The photographer's paperwork (call times, slate numbers, lighting plot, shot log) is the interface, and the pictures are laid at frame scale under it. It refuses the card grid and the dark gallery; images set the layout by bleeding, stepping and overlapping on a numbered schedule. RAISE (jackfield): every frame keeps a durable number. RAISE (slide rack): small fixed type scale, rank by weight and case. RAISE (daylight section): the light is a control and the picture obeys.
OWN-WORLD: Cool paper-white sheet (#ECEEEF family), ink black, one highlighter chartreuse used only as a fill under black text for "your call" marks and active state. Schibsted Grotesk throughout with tabular numerals; huge numerals and tiny ruled legends. Thin ink rules, registration crosses, fold ticks, stamped field labels. Images are full-ink studies of cloth and light with no rounded corners and no shadows.
