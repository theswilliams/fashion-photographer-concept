# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Art directors, magazine photo editors, casting and brand creative leads who are commissioning a fashion photographer, and peers who judge the work. They arrive from an email, a referral or a search, on a laptop at a desk or a phone between meetings, and decide in under a minute whether the eye is right for their shoot. The job: see the work at full size, understand how this photographer thinks, and know how to book.

## Product Purpose
The portfolio site of **Marlowe Adeyemi-Voss**, a fictional fashion and editorial photographer (concept project, portfolio piece 03 of 5). The site exists to show that the work leads: selected series as real separate pages, a fullscreen viewer, a short about, and a booking enquiry. Success is a commissioner who remembers a picture, not the interface, and sends an enquiry.

## Brand Personality
Exacting, quiet, a little severe, with flashes of heat. A working photographer's logic: contact sheets, loupes, grease-pencil marks, edit decisions. The interface recedes; the work is the subject.

## Anti-references
Card grids with rounded thumbnails and hover overlays; generic "minimal portfolio" white page with a serif wordmark; black-and-grey dark gallery with a neon accent; parallax-everything agency sites. Nothing that resembles Project 01 (saturated colour sheets, grotesque plus typewriter mono) or Project 02 (dark warm palette, arched frames, left rail).

## Design Principles
1. The picture is the layout. Frames set the grid, not cards.
2. Edit like a photographer: sequence, crop and scale carry meaning.
3. Interface is sparse and functional; type labels like a frame number, not like decoration.
4. Motion is an edit decision (cut, wipe, advance), not flourish.
5. Honest about its material: every image is labelled as concept imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation including the fullscreen viewer (arrows, Escape, focus trap), alt text on every image, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, no information carried by colour alone.

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vite + vanilla TypeScript, multi-page static output (real separate HTML pages per series), no framework.

## Evidence on Hand
No real photography, clients, publications, awards, testimonials or metrics exist. None may be fabricated or implied. The photographer, studio, series titles, clients and contact details are invented placeholders. Imagery is licensed Unsplash stock photography standing in for the photographer's work, credited on the About page and in the README, to be replaced with original photography.

## Decisions Recorded
- Concept (user choice): fashion photographer.
- Stack (user choice): Vite + vanilla TS, multi-page static.
